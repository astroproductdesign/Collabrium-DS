# Changelog

All notable changes to @collabrium/ui. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.

## Unreleased

### Docs site
- A preview wider than its box (a 280px card on a phone) now scrolls inside the box instead of widening the page.
- Every card picture (home, Motion, Charts (extra), AI Native) is the same 152px box, and what it shows is scaled to fit it (`ThumbFit`), so no preview spills out of its card. Card-layout previews are tinted cards with no text on a fixed canvas.
- The bigger AI Native components (Chat window, Permission Prompt, Tool approval, Task Rows) show outline pictures on their cards; the five hooks (useMousePosition, useReducedMotion, useScrollProgress, useStagger, useWebHaptics) show looping outline pictures. Both are CSS only and still under reduced motion.
- The docs pages fill the width between the sidebar and "On this page" (no 1200px cap; paragraphs keep their 68-character line length), and each example sits in its own tinted panel.
- Preview files for Motion batches 3 to 5 now render every example from their docs file.

### Changed
- Card spread, Card carousel, Card cover flow and Card time machine take a `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`: 0.75, 1 and 1.25 of the designed size) and shrink to fit the space they are in; the piece takes up its scaled size, so nothing hangs outside it.
- `body` now sets body1 (16px / 22px / 500). Charts: gridlines are 1px on both engines, and marks draw in over `--duration-reveal` (Chart.js `easeOutQuart`, Recharts `ease-out`), with reduced motion still off. Bars take 60% of their category (capped at 24px, or 20px horizontal); vertical bar charts (Bar, Stacked bar, Waterfall, Candlestick, Composed) give every category a 40px slot and scroll sideways when they do not fit, with the value axis fixed in place; axis numbers shorten (500k, 1.2M) while tooltips show the full value, tooltips are per category for line, area and bar charts and per point or slice for the rest, and the legend swatch is 12px.

### Added
- Feature tile (USP) in `Card`: `minWidth="functional"` (280px), `CardList` (three check-circle points) and a Button as a direct child, stretched to the full width of the card and pinned to the bottom of every card in a row.
- `Card` can be selectable: give it a `selectLabel` and it carries the real Checkbox on the left (shown on hover, keyboard focus and while selected, and always on touch), with a Selected look of a 2px Obsidian border and a deeper shadow. For rosters with a bulk action.
- `Card`, `CardTitle`, `CardHeader`, `CardFooter`, `CardGrid` and `IconChip`: the base card (surface, header with an icon chip and a subtitle, footer, element accent and tint, warm fill) and the grid it sits in. A clickable card is a real link or button in the title, stretched over the card. The docs site now uses them for its own cards.
- Motion, batch 5: `CardSpread` (eight layouts), `CardCarousel`, `CardCoverFlow` and `CardTimeMachine`, in `@collabrium/ui/motion`. Cards are items with the DLS surface; the controls are DLS Buttons and dots; only the front card is exposed to assistive technology.
- Motion, batch 4: `IconSwap`, `CountUp`, `ScrambleHover`, `FocusBlur`, `TextSweep`, `MediaBetweenText`, `PlayPauseMorph`, `SearchExpand`, `ExpandingTabs` and `Dock`, in `@collabrium/ui/motion`. Effects keep the real text in the page for assistive technology; the tabs are a keyboard-operable tab list.
- Motion, batch 3: `ScrollProgress`, `ScrollReveal`, `StickyReveal`, `PageTransition` (stairs, wave, fade) and the hooks `useScrollProgress`, `useMousePosition`, `useStagger`, `useWebHaptics` and `useReducedMotion`, in `@collabrium/ui/motion`. Reduced motion keeps the fade or follows the scroll exactly; every stagger and duration is a token.
- Motion, batch 2: text effects (`BlurText`, `CharacterStagger`, `WordReveal`, `TextReveal`), hover effects (`HoverCards`, `Spotlight`, `Magnetic`, `Tilt`) and cursor effects (`CursorTrail`, `MouseFollow`), in `@collabrium/ui/motion`. Text is read once as a sentence; reduced motion keeps the fade and drops the rest.
- Motion, batch 1: `SlideDown`, `SlideLeft` and `SlideRight` (in `@collabrium/ui/motion`), and pages for `FadeIn`, `SlideUp` and `Collapse`. The slides share one drawing, and the root export lists only the three original wrappers.
- Charts (extra), batch 6: `ComposedChart` (bars and a line, with an optional second axis) and `SparklineChart` (the DLS sparkline, with a spoken trend summary).
- Charts (extra), batch 5: `RadarChart`, `PolarAreaChart`, `SankeyChart`, `CandlestickChart` (Recharts) and `PyramidChart` (HTML and CSS). DLS Radar and Polar area marks, a computed Sankey, and candlesticks that tell direction by solid or hollow as well as colour.
- Charts (extra), batch 4: `ScatterChart`, `BubbleChart`, `TreemapChart` (Recharts) and `HeatmapChart`, `ActivityHeatmapChart` (HTML and CSS). Single-hue shading for the heatmaps and treemap, axis titles on scatter and bubble, and a table that stays in the page, visually hidden, for charts whose picture has no text.
- `ChartThumbnail` in `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra`: draws any chart as a bare picture of its shape, for cards and galleries. The Charts (extra) overview uses it for its part cards.
- Charts (extra), batch 3: `DonutChart`, `RadialBarGroupChart`, `RadialGaugeChart`, `GaugeArcChart` and `MeterChart` (Recharts). The DLS Doughnut look (2px white border, same-hue ramp), a centre readout, and gauges driven by `value` and `max`. The sequential ramp now has four rungs (`rampRungs`). The centre readout is an h5 (16px) value over a caption.
- Charts (extra), batch 2: `BarChart`, `StackedBarChart`, `WaterfallChart`, `FunnelChart` (Recharts) and `BulletChart` (HTML and CSS). DLS bar rules (outer-end radius, 24px/20px thickness caps, hover mix, value axis from zero), stack remainder in Neutral-3, a single-hue ramp, and a waterfall in Green, Red and Navy. The frame now splits into `ChartShell` and `CartesianFrame`.
- Charts (extra), batch 1, in `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` (Recharts, optional peer dependency): `LineChart`, `AreaChart`, `StreamChart`, `StepChart`, `RangeChart`. DLS chrome, marks and series colours; a legend that shows or hides a series; "View as table" on every chart; reduced motion draws the final chart. Docs: the new Charts (extra) and Motion groups.
- Entry points `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` (Recharts, for shapes Chart.js cannot draw; optional peer dependency `recharts`) and `@collabrium/ui/motion`. `charts-extra` never imports Chart.js and `charts` never imports Recharts. `charts-extra` shares the DLS series colours (`seriesColors`).
- Package scaffold:
  - Build: tsup builds one ES module per source file, so each file keeps its `"use client"`, plus type declarations and a single `dist/styles.css`.
  - Two entry points: `@collabrium/ui` and `@collabrium/ui/charts`.
- Tokens:
  - `tokens.css` is copied from DLS v0.9.124, with the same names and values.
  - `tokens.ts` is generated from it, with a drift check.
- Base styles: box-sizing, canvas, font, text colour.
- Motion: `motion.ts` (token durations and easings), plus `FadeIn`, `SlideUp` and `Collapse` on the `motion` library.
- Charts: `setupCharts()` applies Chart.js defaults from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md "Chart chrome & marks".
- Playground: a Next.js App Router app with an index page and one preview page per component. It has a static export that opens from disk.
- **Button** (in review). The props cover:
  - Variants: Primary, Secondary, Ghost.
  - Sizes: sm, md, lg.
  - Icons: leading icon, trailing arrow, icon-only.
  - States: disabled, loading.
  - CTA beacon: on and static.
- **Badge** (in review). Five tones (Neutral, Success, Error, Warning, Selected) and an optional leading icon. Info is not built yet (no CSS in the DLS).
- **Checkbox** (in review). Real native checkbox with a label, an optional description, a partial (indeterminate) state, disabled, and a keyboard focus ring. Radio and Switch (same CSS block in the DLS) come next.
- **Loading State** (in review), the first of AI Native's eight parts. Drive, Dots and Orbit variants, a live label and a real elapsed timer. It counts from a start timestamp and keeps a polite live region (the host mounts it after 400ms); reduced motion freezes the grid and keeps the timer.
- **Thinking** (in review), the second of AI Native's eight parts. An expandable agent trace with four variants (Steps, Reasoning, Search, Coding). It opens itself while the run is working, closes when done, and the reader's click wins from then on; exactly one spinner at a time; the closed trace is inert.
- **Streaming Text** (in review), the third of AI Native's eight parts. The model's prose arriving word by word through a buffer, with a blinking caret and citation chips landing in the sentence. Once the last word settles the action row (Copy, Retry, thumbs), a sources list and follow-ups appear; a stopped answer keeps its text. A message that arrives already finished is shown whole. Failed streams and tables, code and lists are not built (no DLS markup).
- **Tokens, 2026-10-26.** Added to the DLS `tokens.css` (and copied here): focus ring, control sizes, status tints and AA text colours for Green and Amber (red text stays `--color-red`, 3.2:1, by decision), `--spacing-2` and `--spacing-6`, `--radius-xs`, measures, pulse and blink durations, staggers and prose leading. The components now use them in place of their local values (computed styles were identical before and after), and the Foundation pages show them. New: `--icon-xs` (12px) and `--color-red-text` (#D4001A), now used for red text in Badge Error, Checkbox and Thinking.
- **Review round, 2026-10-26.** Button: icons 16px at every size, `aria-disabled` look, forced-colours edge on Primary and Danger, new `danger` and `link` variants. Badge: 22px, caption type, 14px icons, darker Success and Warning text, new `info` tone. Checkbox: 14px glyph, hover and pressed, `required`, `error` and `CheckboxGroup`. Loading State: grid visible in forced colours, long labels end in an ellipsis, and the 400ms hold-back is now the host's job (the component renders as soon as it mounts). Button: the fill is fixed while loading. Thinking: running timer (`startedAt`, `{duration}`), announcement outside the button, Obsidian focus ring on rows, spinner keeps turning under reduced motion. Task Rows: `stopped` status; Retrying arrow keeps turning. Streaming Text: `failed` status.
- **Task Rows** (in review), the fourth of AI Native's eight parts. The named tasks of an agent run as rows with a status disc, count, pill and droppable detail steps, in two variants (Capsules, List). One row open at a time: a failure (or the task now in progress) opens itself and the reader's click wins. Retry shows once, on a retryable failure only, and is never disabled. Six rows then "+N more". Failures, a retry and the end of the run are announced politely.
- **Permission Prompt** (in review), the fifth of AI Native's eight parts. A card the AI puts in its own turn to ask authorization for one specific action: icon, question, risk badge (Low, Medium, High), a "What this will do" detail of key and value rows, a scope note, and three actions (Deny, Allow once, Allow for session). Four statuses set by the host: pending, allowed, denied, expired; a resolved card stays as the record. Under 768px the actions restack with Allow for session on top. Focus moves to the resolved row when a keyboard decision removes the focused button.
- **Radio** (in review). `Radio`: a native radio in its label, an outline circle with a dot that never fills solid, a label and an optional description; hover, pressed, a focus ring and a dot that shows in forced colours are added. `RadioGroup`: a fieldset with a legend, a shared name, `value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange`, `required`, a helper or error line, and a red border on unselected circles when in error. Radio Card (built on Card) is not converted yet.
- **Input field** (in review). `InputField`: a single-line text box with a label (always rendered, `hideLabel` for assistive tech only), helper or error line, required marker, success state, sizes sm, md and lg, leading and trailing icons, prefix and suffix text, and a character count. Focus is a 2px Obsidian border swap. The box fills its container. Textarea, Password field and Search input (which extend it) are not converted yet.
- **Textarea** (in review). `Textarea`: a multi-line box that extends Input field: the same label (always rendered, `hideLabel` for assistive tech), helper or error line, required marker, 1px Red error border, Neutral-2 disabled look and 2px Obsidian focus border. At least 96px tall and resizes vertically only. The box fills its container. An optional character count (`showCount` with `maxLength`), off unless you set it.
- **Tool approval** (in review), the sixth of AI Native's eight parts. A card the AI shows mid-task for decisions only the person can make: one question at a time, single-choice (radios, moves on by itself) or multiple-choice (checkboxes, waits for Continue), an always-available written answer that opens into a Textarea, a step count with Previous and Next, Skip, Continue or Send, and a dismiss ×. Options and the written answer exclude each other; Continue and Send need an answer; the height morphs between questions; it closes for good however it closes and renders nothing after. Built on the library's Radio, Checkbox, Input field, Textarea and Button.
- **Prompt input bar** (in review), the seventh of AI Native's eight parts. `PromptInputBar`: a card with one text box and a toolbar (add image, attach file, more options, a model switcher, voice input, Send). `variant="floating"`: pinned over a region, fades in once, collapses to a pill when the region scrolls past 240px (and expands within 8px), types its resting line once, opens the chat when pressed; `parkShift` and `pushShift` take measured sideways offsets. `variant="inline"`: in flow as a chat composer, no collapse, no mark, a plain hint. The model switcher opens an upward menu in a portal. `working` turns Send into Stop.
- **Chip** (in review). `FilterChipGroup` (single-select radio group or multi-select checkbox group with a tick, an optional category icon, a trailing count, disabled chips) and `InputChipGroup` (removable value chips, static chips, "+N" overflow with "Show less"). Built first because Chat window's pillar filter uses it.
- **Chat window** (in review), the eighth and last of AI Native's parts. `ChatWindow` (the modal: it folds out of the floating bar, a rail with the pillar filter and conversations that becomes a drawer below 820px, the thread with Jump to present and the scroll pin, an inline Prompt input bar as the composer) and `ChatMessage` (the person's bubble, and the AI's plain copy with citations and the action row: copy, read aloud, thumbs, more options). The Prompt input bar's model menu now draws in the chat window's popover layer when it sits in one, and on a very narrow inline composer its name shrinks so Send is never clipped. Streaming Text gains `actions` (set `false` inside a `ChatMessage`), with a new example.
- **Switch** (in review). A real native switch (`role="switch"`) with a label, an optional description, off, on and disabled. Hover and pressed (the thumb's shadow), the focus ring, forced-colours and reduced-motion rules are designed here (the DLS has none).
- Docs site: **AI Native is one group.** The sidebar and home grid show a single "AI Native" item. Its overview page explains the family and links to each part (Loading State, Thinking) at `components/ai-native/<part>`; the parts the DLS lists but that are not converted yet show as "Coming soon". Previous and next walk through the overview and its parts.
- Docs site: link clicks inside a preview do nothing, so no demo can navigate away.
- Docs site: every component page now shows all its sections. A section with no content yet says "Coming soon." Only `title` and `description` are required in a docs file.
