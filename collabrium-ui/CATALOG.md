# Catalog

What to use for each need. Only converted components are listed; the full
list of components still to convert is in MIGRATION-LOG.md.

| Need | Use | Don't use |
|---|---|---|
| Start an action on the current page (save, add, open, continue) | `Button`: `variant="primary"` for the one main action in a region, `"secondary"` beside it, `"ghost"` for low-emphasis, toolbar and table-row actions | A link styled as a button; a second Primary in the same region; an element/department colour as the fill |
| A compact action with no visible label | `Button iconOnly icon={…} aria-label="…"` (Tier 1 icon) | An icon with no `aria-label`; a Fill (Tier 2) icon |
| Name an icon button on hover, or add one short fact to a control | `Tooltip content="…"` around one focusable element; `role="label"` when it is the trigger's only name; `placement` only when top doesn't suit | More than 60 characters; links or buttons inside; essential information; a tooltip on something that can't take focus |
| Show that an action is running | `Button loading` | Disabling the button; swapping the label for "Loading…" |
| Commit a destructive action (delete, revoke), or a text-level action inside a line of content | `Button variant="danger"` for the committing step of a destructive flow; `Button variant="link"` for "Show less" and similar | Danger for anything non-destructive or with the beacon; Link for a real link to another page; the opening "Delete…" step as Danger |
| Point to the single next step after something finishes | `Button variant="primary" beacon="on" trailingArrow="down"` | Beacon on page load or a timer; more than one beacon on a page; beacon on Secondary, Ghost, icon-only or disabled |
| Show the status or tone of something (Active, Pending, Failed) | `Badge variant="success" \| "warning" \| "error" \| "neutral"`; `"selected"` for the chosen item in a set | A Badge for ownership (use Tag); a clickable or removable Badge (use Chip or Button); a solid brand fill with white text |
| Say which element (department) owns something (Fire · Marketing), or label a category with no department | `Tag variant="fire" \| "wood" \| "earth" \| "water" \| "gold"`; `"purple"` for a category with no department; `"neutral"` for a plain label | A Tag for a status (that is Badge); a clickable or removable Tag (use Chip); an element that doesn't own the content |
| Mark which element owns an item with the element's own icon (a team row, an owner column, an introduction to the five elements) | `ElementBadge element="fire" \| "earth" \| "water" \| "gold" \| "wood"`; `showLabel` for the name and function beside it, `sublabel` for something more specific | A recoloured chip; an ElementBadge for a status (that is Badge) or for a non-element category (that is Tag); Card's icon chip outside a card header |
| Let the user pick any number of options, or switch one setting on or off and save it with a button | `Checkbox label="…"`; `indeterminate` only on a select-all box; `aria-label` when no visible label fits (a table row) | A Checkbox when only one option can be chosen (Radio) or when the setting applies instantly (Switch); a bare box with no name |
| Let the user pick exactly one option from a short set (up to four or five visible options) | `RadioGroup legend="…"` with `Radio value="…" label="…"` inside; `value` and `onValueChange` to control it; `required` and `error` for a group that needs a choice | A Radio when several options can be on (Checkbox) or when there are more than five (a select); mixing Radio and Checkbox in one group; an instant view switch (Segmented Control); Radio Card (needs Card, not converted yet) |
| Let the user flip one setting on or off and have it apply at once (no Save step) | `Switch label="…"` (what it controls, never its state); `checked` and `onChange`, or `defaultChecked`; `aria-label` when no visible label fits | A Switch in a form that needs a Save (Checkbox); picking one of several (Radio); picking several (Checkbox); a label that says "On" or "Off" |
| Group related content into a tile or panel (dashboard tile, list item), optionally with an icon chip, a footer, or a clickable whole card | `CardGrid` > `Card` with `CardHeader` / `CardTitle` / `CardFooter`; `accent` or `tint` with an element only for content that element owns; `href` or `onClick` on the title to make the whole card clickable | A fixed card width; a link or button wrapped around the whole card; a checkbox on a card with no bulk action (that is the Selectable card, not built yet) |
| Let people tick cards in a roster that a bulk action (a toolbar, "Delete 3 selected") acts on | `Card selectLabel="Select …"` with `selected` and `onSelectedChange`, inside a `CardGrid`; the title keeps the card's own `href` or `onClick` | A checkbox on a card with no bulk action (leave out `selectLabel`); choosing one card from a group (Radio Card) |
| Show a feature tile: a title, a description, three points and one call to action | `CardGrid columns="functional"` > `Card minWidth="functional"` with `CardTitle`, `<p>`, `CardList items={[…three…]}` and a `Button variant="primary"` as a direct child | Building the button from scratch or wrapping it; more than three points; a fixed card width |
| Show the line items someone has picked, their costs and a total, with one next step (a quote, a proposal) | `CardGrid columns="functional"` > `PriceSummaryCard items={[…]} onRemove={…} onAction={…}`; `removable: false` for a locked item; `actionLabel` for the step | Rounded summary figures (a budget); your own "Show more" or total row; more than one button in its footer |
| Show one person (talent, influencer) across their platforms, with reach and tier, in a roster | `CardGrid` > `ProfileCard name="…" age gender location personas={[…]} platforms={[{ platform, followers, href }]}`; `selectable` for a bulk action; `href` or `onOpen` to open the person; `onMenuAction` for the kebab | One card per account; a hand-picked ranked tier; a colour per avatar |
| Show one campaign as a tile on a kanban board, where the column is the stage | `CampaignCard name brand owner overseer dates={{ start, end }} pax quote href` in a column; `lead` for a lead; `dragging` while the board drags it; `movable={false}` for a viewer who can only open | A stage tag on the tile; 0 for an unset value; the base Card's border or equal height; a table view (it needs a stage cell) |
| Describe an ad slot on a placement map while it is pointed at or focused (what it is, sizes, devices, how many units fit) | `AdPlacementCard icon title where description sizes={[…]} devices={[…]} fit={{ count, total, built }} cta` wrapped around the slot's `<button>` | Links or buttons inside; a format rather than a position (Media Ad Card); a one-line hint (Tooltip); relying on it on touch |
| Show a total (or its parts) against a named target, and how much is missing | `GoalBar title target value` (one value) or `categories={[{ label, value }]}`; `variant="chart"` inside a surface that owns its container; `variant="table"` in a table cell | A plain percentage with no target (Progress Bar); several categories in a `size="small"` bar; a Target entry in the legend |
| Show one metric racing a deadline to a target (a channel's spend against its monthly budget) | `CardGrid` > `PacingCard label kpi kpiQualifier value target day days metOnDay` | No target or no time boundary (Stat / KPI card); a trend row; a third badge colour; a category breakdown on the rail |
| Offer a short menu of starting points, each opening one flow (Brief, Research, Deck…) | `EntryCardSet label variant="artefact" \| "character"` > `EntryCard title hint artefact onClick` (or `href`) | Mixing variants in a set; a promo with body copy (Hero Card); something to buy (Media Ad Card); an `aria-label` on the card |
| Tell the reader something about the section or card they're in: a state, a limit, a tip, or an AI suggestion | `InfoBanner tone="info"` (default) at the top of the section; `dismissible` when it isn't critical; `actions={[…]}` (two at most); `tone="ai"` with exactly one action | Feedback on something just done (Toast); a destructive action; more than two per section, or two AI banners |
| Surface one announcement, risk or AI insight worth acting on, with a detail view | `HeroCard variant="default" \| "risk" \| "ai" eyebrow heading body media cta ribbon`; `onOpen` + `CommandBrief open onClose` for the detail; `variant="selected"` for the detail in the page | Ordinary content (Card); a Ghost or Primary CTA on the AI card; a ribbon on AI or Selected |
| Show one ad format (or placement, creative template) as a tile someone can open and compare | `CardGrid columns="functional"` > `MediaAdCard name family tagline funnel objective sizes benchmark onOpen onComparedChange`; `MediaAdCardSkeleton` while loading | A tile with no media (interactive Card); dropping a spec row when a value is missing; an ad visual inside the component; family colours as departments |
| Show one item whose figures matter most (an audience segment, influencer, channel) as a tile someone can open and add to a tray | `CardGrid columns="functional"` > `MetricsCard name category categoryTag tagline size unit totalLabel bars addLabel onOpen onAddedChange`; `MetricsCardSkeleton` while loading | Sorting the bars; dropping a bar with no value; labels over 13 characters; category colours as departments; a split that is the point (Progress-to-Goal Bar) |
| Show progress toward a known goal, a breakdown, or a task of unknown length | `ProgressBar title value` (`tone="success" \| "error"`, `size`, `labelPlacement`); `segments` for a breakdown; `indeterminate` | Moving a bar backward; a department colour as the default fill; a label inside a compact or stacked bar; letting it turn green by itself at 100% |
| Show that a page, a section or a search has nothing in it, or that its data failed to load | `EmptyState variant="section"` (or `"fullpage"`) with `icon`, `heading`, the body as children, `primaryAction` and `secondaryAction`; `tone="error"` with Retry when loading failed; `variant="inline"` with `linkAction` for a search or filter with no results | `tone="empty"` over a broken fetch; a bare "No data"; a spinner or blank card in its place; more than two actions; a Primary or Ghost button inline |
| Ask one decision over the page before going on (archive, delete, discard changes) | `Modal open onClose title="…?"` with the body as children and `actions` (Secondary `Button` then Primary) | A modal for a long form or a multi-step flow (use a page); for a passing message (Toast, Info Banner); two Primary buttons |
| Show how far along a multi-step flow someone is (onboarding, a multi-page form, a checkout) | `Stepper steps={[{ label }]} currentStep={n}`; `orientation="vertical"` in a side panel; a step's `flag: 'error'` (current step) or `'warning'` (completed step) with its message as `description` | A state tracked per step; an Error on an Upcoming step; Stepper for a plan whose items each have a status (Timeline) or for a − / + field (Quantity selector) |
| Go one level up from a drill-down screen (a report opened from Reports) | `BackButton label="Reports" href="/reports"` (or `onClick`), above the page header | A generic "Back"; a breadcrumb trail or Home shortcut; a Back button on a Level 1 screen; one inside the page header |
| Title a page (or a full panel), with an optional line of context and page-wide actions | `PageHeader title subtitle actions={<Button …/>}`; a `BackButton` above it on Level 2+ screens | Two PageHeaders in one view; one as a card or section header; a border, fill or divider on it; the Back button inside it |
| Switch between up to ~6 sections of different content on one page | `Tabs aria-label tabs={[{ id, label, count?, icon?, content }]}`; `value` / `onValueChange` to keep the tab in a URL parameter | Tabs to change how the same content is shown (Segmented Control); more than ~6 tabs (SidebarNav, a Select); icon-only tabs; a tab bar inside a panel |
| Switch between 2–5 views of the same content (List / Grid / Board, MTD / QTD / YTD), inline in a header or toolbar | `SegmentedControl aria-label options={[{ value, label }]}` (or all with `icon`); `size="compact"` in toolbars | More than 5 options (Tabs, a Select); icon and text options mixed; a form choice saved later (Radio); an element colour on the selected segment |
| Filter a list or view by a few predefined options (status, department, pillar), or show the values a person has added to a field | `FilterChipGroup mode="single" or "multi"`, `aria-label`, `options` (`value`, `onValueChange`); `InputChipGroup aria-label chips onRemove` (folds past 3 into "+N") | A chip to label a status or owner (Badge, Tag); a filter chip as a form Checkbox; mixing input and filter chips or single and multi in one group; a filter row that wraps past two lines (Dropdown, multiple select) |
| Open a full conversation with the AI from the floating Prompt input bar: history, a thread, a composer | `ChatWindow open onClose origin title pillars groups composer` with `ChatMessage role="user"` or `"assistant"` as children; set the bar's `launched` to `open` | An inline chat in a page (it is a modal); a close button; a custom message layout; anything but `ChatMessage` in the thread |
| Let the user type one line of text, an email, a URL, a phone number, a number or a password | `InputField label="…" helper="…"`; `required`, `error`, `success`, `size`, `leadingIcon` / `trailingIcon`, `prefix` / `suffix`, `showCount` with `maxLength`; `type="password"` with `autoComplete="current-password"` (or `new-password`) for a password, with its show/hide toggle | A placeholder instead of a label; "(required)" or "(optional)" in the label; a pressable prefix or suffix (icon button); a quantity with − and + (Quantity selector); a search or several lines (their own components); a success check or suffix on a password |
| Let the user search: filter the page by text, or find and pick one person or record | `SearchInput label="Search" onValueChange` (Default); `SearchInput variant="user" \| "item" query onQueryChange results={[{ id, title, subtitle, initials }]} loading onSelectedChange` to pick one | A clear button and another trailing icon together; a full Empty state for "No matches found"; a Fill spinner; Search input for a short, known list (Dropdown, Radio) |
| Show a vertical run of same-shaped rows: pages to open, figures in a card, a who-did-what feed, settings with switches | `List aria-labelledby` with `ListItem heading=…` rows: `href` (+ `chevron`) to open a page, `onClick` to act in place, `control={{ type: 'switch' }}` to toggle, none for static; `leading`, `description`, `caption`, `value`, `badge`, `action`; `ListSection` for day headers, `ListItemSkeleton` in a `busy` list, `ListEmpty` / `ListError` / `ListFooter` | A chevron on a button, static or activity row; a timestamp in trailing; an unread dot; options in a popover (Dropdown); columns (Table); a one-row list on a dashboard (a Stat card) |
| Let the user write several lines (a description, a note, a comment) | `Textarea label="…" helper="…"`; `required`, `error`, `showCount` with `maxLength`; it resizes vertically only, from 96px | A Textarea for one line (Input field); a placeholder instead of a label; horizontal resizing; a quick reply in a conversation (Prompt input bar) |
| Ask a question that takes one or more checkboxes, with a helper, a required marker and a group error | `CheckboxGroup legend="…" required error={…}` around `Checkbox`es; `required` and `error` on a single `Checkbox` for a must-tick box | Several checkboxes with no legend; `required` on every box in a group; an error left showing after a selection |
| Show that an AI operation is running before any result has arrived (model generation, tool execution, agent runs) | `LoadingState variant="orbit" label="Thinking" startedAt={…}`; update `label` as the phase changes; unmount it at the first streamed token; give the surface a Stop | A LoadingState for a data fetch, upload or table load (spinner or skeleton); two in one view; one that blocks the page or survives the first token |
| Show the named steps of an AI run as an expandable record (steps, reasoning, sources read, tool calls) once two or more steps exist and two seconds have passed | `Thinking variant="steps" \| "reasoning" \| "search" \| "coding"` with `status`, `workingLabel`, `doneLabel`; give the surface a Stop; hand off from `LoadingState` and never back | A Thinking before the first named step lands, with one step, or for work under two seconds (use `LoadingState`); two in one view; a variant chosen by preference |
| Show an AI answer arriving as it is produced, with citations in the sentence and actions once it settles | `StreamingText content={…} status="streaming" \| "done" \| "stopped"`; append to `content` as text arrives; start it at the first token and remove the Loading State or Thinking there | A StreamingText over text you already hold (cached, replayed, computed); a stream for a table, code or a list; a second one for the same answer; one that scrolls the page |
| Show an AI run of several named tasks, each with its own status, where one can fail while the others succeed | `TaskRows tasks={…} onRetry={…}`; `variant="list"` inside a card or panel; set `retryable` only on a transient failure | A TaskRows for one operation with ordered steps (use `Thinking`), data loading, or a checklist; a disabled Retry; a control on the row for something the reader must supply (ask in the conversation); two lists in one view |
| Ask the person's authorization before the AI does one specific, consequential action (read or write a file, run a command, send a message, call an outside service) | `PermissionPrompt status={…} risk={…} icon={…} details={…} onDeny={…} onAllowOnce={…} onAllowSession={…}`, in the AI's own turn; set `status` from the decision; set `expired` only when the conversation moves on | A PermissionPrompt for something that only affects the conversation, or to ask a question or get a decision (Tool approval); an overlay or modal; raw JSON or a truncated value in the detail; removing a resolved card; a control that sets Expired |
| Get one or more decisions from the person before the AI's task can go on (one question at a time, options plus a written answer) | `ToolApproval questions={…} onSend={…} onDismiss={…}`; `single` questions use radios and move on by themselves, `multiple` use checkboxes; raise a new instance for a later decision | A ToolApproval for authorizing a specific action (Permission Prompt); a general form; stacking two; disabling or hiding the dismiss; leaving a confirmation or a reopen button behind |
| Let the person ask the AI: a text box with a toolbar and a model switcher | `PromptInputBar variant="floating"` (pinned over a region, collapses on scroll with `scrollContainer`, opens the chat with `onLaunch`) or `variant="inline"` (a chat window's composer); set `working` to turn Send into Stop | A PromptInputBar for a form field (Input field, Textarea); a scroll-collapse, a mark or the typing line on the inline variant; opening the chat from focus; hard-coded park or push offsets; a popover inside the bar with absolute positioning |
| Fade content in and out | `FadeIn` | CSS keyframes per screen |
| Slide content up into place | `SlideUp` | Custom transforms with other distances or springs |
| Expand and collapse a region | `Collapse` | `max-height` hacks |
| Chart.js defaults in brand style | `setupCharts()` from `@collabrium/ui/charts` | Hand-set chart fonts and colours |
| Draw a line, area, stream, step or range chart when Chart.js does not fit | `LineChart`, `AreaChart`, `StreamChart`, `StepChart`, `RangeChart` from `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` (Recharts; `aria-label`, `data`, `categoryKey`, `series`; each has a table view) | Mixing both chart sets in one chart; a chart title inside the chart (the Card names it); colour as the only difference between series |
| Compare values across categories, parts within a total, a bridge between two totals, stages of a funnel, or measures against targets when Chart.js does not fit | `BarChart`, `StackedBarChart`, `WaterfallChart`, `FunnelChart`, `BulletChart` from `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` (`BulletChart` is HTML and CSS) | Bar for a bridge between totals (Waterfall); a stack that does not sum to its label (`totalKey`); a ramp for different categories; Bullet for many categories |
| Show part of a whole as a ring, several measures against their limits, or one value on a dial when Chart.js does not fit | `DonutChart` (up to four slices), `RadialBarGroupChart`, `RadialGaugeChart`, `GaugeArcChart`, `MeterChart` from `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` | A donut with more than four slices (Bar); a gauge to compare two values (Bar); a free colour for slices (`color` picks a hue, the slices walk its ramp) |
| Plot two or three measures against each other, a pattern across two categories, parts of a total as areas, or a calendar of daily counts when Chart.js does not fit | `ScatterChart`, `BubbleChart`, `HeatmapChart`, `TreemapChart`, `ActivityHeatmapChart` from `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` | A scatter line between points; a rainbow ramp (one hue); a bubble chart with more than three series |
| Compare profiles across dimensions, volume by unordered category, levels of a hierarchy, flow between stages, or open-high-low-close per period when Chart.js does not fit | `RadarChart`, `PolarAreaChart`, `PyramidChart`, `SankeyChart`, `CandlestickChart` from `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` | A "polar" chart of rings (Radial gauge); a sankey with a loop; colour alone for up and down in a candlestick |
| Bars with a line over them for two measures of the same categories, or a glance at a trend beside a figure, when Chart.js does not fit | `ComposedChart`, `SparklineChart` from `@collabrium/ui/charts-extra` | A sparkline with axes or a legend; a sparkline alone (use Line chart); a headline number as a chart (use Stat / KPI card) |
| Fade, slide or open content in and out, by token duration and easing | `FadeIn`, `SlideUp`, `Collapse` from `@collabrium/ui`; also `SlideDown`, `SlideLeft`, `SlideRight` from `@collabrium/ui/motion` | Your own numbers for duration or distance; a loader (use Loading State); bounce or overshoot |
| Reveal a heading letter by letter, word by word or line by line; make a card or button answer the pointer; or add a playful cursor | `BlurText`, `CharacterStagger`, `WordReveal`, `TextReveal`, `HoverCards`, `Spotlight`, `Magnetic`, `Tilt`, `CursorTrail`, `MouseFollow` from `@collabrium/ui/motion` | Body text in a text effect; a trail or follower on a product screen; your own spring or duration numbers |
| Show reading progress, reveal sections on scroll, tell a scroll story with a sticky panel, cover a page swap, or drive your own motion (scroll position, mouse position, stagger delays, vibration, reduced motion) | `ScrollProgress`, `ScrollReveal`, `StickyReveal`, `PageTransition`, `useScrollProgress`, `useMousePosition`, `useStagger`, `useWebHaptics`, `useReducedMotion` from `@collabrium/ui/motion` | A toggle (use Switch); a transition that hides a slow load (use Loading State); your own stagger numbers |
| Swap an icon, count a figure, scramble or sweep a heading, put a picture in a phrase, morph play to pause, grow a search, open a tab's label or swell a row of icons | `IconSwap`, `CountUp`, `ScrambleHover`, `FocusBlur`, `TextSweep`, `MediaBetweenText`, `PlayPauseMorph`, `SearchExpand`, `ExpandingTabs`, `Dock` from `@collabrium/ui/motion` | A search that is the page's main action; page navigation in Expanding tabs; reordering in Dock |
| Preview a stack of cards that fans out, browse cards in a sliding row or in 3D, or step back through a deck with a scrubber | `CardSpread`, `CardCarousel`, `CardCoverFlow`, `CardTimeMachine` from `@collabrium/ui/motion` | Cards that hold controls; a list the person must scan; your own spring numbers |
