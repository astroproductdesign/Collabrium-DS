Changes: (1) Completed is now unfilled with a --border-width-sm Obsidian border and a tick icon instead of the step number; Active is now filled Obsidian with the step number in Neutral-1 (the swap of what Completed used to look like) — Upcoming is unchanged. (2) Horizontal's connector no longer depends on label width at all: .c-step is now pinned to width:24px (the indicator's own width) instead of sizing to its label's natural width, so .c-step-connector's flex:1 always fills exactly the space between two 24px columns, with a fixed margin:0 var(--spacing-4) gap against the indicator on each side — never a hardcoded pixel width, never a gap that grows or shrinks with the label next to it. (3) Connector stroke weight and indicator border weight now use the two new tokens in tokens.css, --border-width-sm (1px) and --border-width-md (unused here but reserved) — no more literal 1px/2px. Vertical was already architecturally isolated from label width (its rail is a separate sibling from the text), so only its border/stroke tokens changed, not its structure.
