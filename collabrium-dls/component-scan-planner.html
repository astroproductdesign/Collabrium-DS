Two corrections to the scan table first

I checked components.css again before drawing these, and two things I called new are already in the system. The coach-mark tour is Modal's Tutorial variant — .c-modal-tutorial ships the card, the kicker, the spotlight layer and the same grow-out-of-the-target entrance the planner hand-rolls as .tw-card. The stacked share bar is Progress Bar's stacked track plus its documented legend — .c-progress-track.stacked, .c-progress-stack-segment with the department tones, and .c-progress-legend. The planner uses the DS bar for audience pull and a private copy for budget by channel. Both move to reuse, so this page shows 21 builds, not 23.

The AI signature

Four surfaces wearing one recipe: a pastel sweep washed under the content at low opacity, plus a 1px gradient rim on the same animation. The planner pastes the gradient four times. My recommendation is that this is one token-level recipe the system defines once, then applies to Badge, Button, Banner and Field — not four components.
