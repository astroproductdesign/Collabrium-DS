How a row changes

Suggested → queued when you confirm the scan table. Queued → drafted when the component-builder subagent saves [component-name]-draft.html; the filename goes in the Draft column. Drafted → promoted when the component lands in components.css and design-system.md. Anything → skipped when you say no — the row stays so the same suggestion is not made twice.
