Metrics card

One item someone can pick or reach, as a gallery tile. At rest it shows media, a name, a category Tag, a tagline and a size line. Live, it shows a headline total and a breakdown of up to five bars. First use case: audience segments on Collab:Media's audiences page. It could also serve influencers, channels or campaigns. It's a variant of the shipped Media Ad Card: c-card c-card-interactive c-card-media-ad c-card-media-ad-metrics. Frame, slot, Live state, round toggle, title, swap and focus all come from the shipped card. This variant adds a slot contract for a still with a hover clip, a fixed 2-line title area, a size line, and the total + bars Live face. Data-specific words (the size unit, the total's label, the toggle's label, the bar labels) are supplied by the host. The demo data on this page is audiences, so the examples read "audience", "total audience" and "Add {Name} to reach tray". Every card shows the shipped media placeholder: no art is rendered.

Why a Media Ad Card variant, not a new card
  • It's the same interaction: a planner sweeps the pointer across a gallery and the details appear over the card. Same Live triggers, same first-tap-goes-Live on touch, same two-button structure.
  • The same anatomy: full-bleed 16:10 media, a round toggle top-left, title + Tag, then a fixed-height body that swaps.
  • A second card would copy about 150 lines of shipped CSS and drift from it. As a modifier, it picks up every fix made to the shipped card automatically.
What differs from formats
  • Toggle: an Add toggle with a host-supplied fixed label (audiences: "Add {Name} to reach tray") instead of Compare. Same look and contract; pressed = .is-added.
  • Tag names the item's category, not a format family. For audiences it's mapped from the character bible's body colours.
  • Title row: the title area is always 2 lines tall (ellipsis on line 2), and the Tag stays on one line, with a 13-character label limit (capped at 120px, which fits every approved label in full), so the heading is never squeezed and every card is the same height.
  • Rest: tagline (3 lines, not 2) + a size line, "{Size} {unit} · {top %} {top bar}" (audiences: "16M audience · 66% social"), instead of funnel stages.
  • Live: a total + 1–5 bars (shipped Progress Bar) instead of the three Objective/Sizes/Benchmark rows. The swap is taller to fit five. See Bar count for 0, fewer than 5, and more than 5.
  • Media: a still + a muted looping clip that plays only while Live. Media Ad Card leaves media fully to the host.

Bar count

What happens when the host has something other than five bars. All specimens are static and Live, with demo audience data. Every card is the same height: the swap keeps the 5-row height whatever the count, so equal heights in a grid always win.

Rule
  • 1–5 bars: shown in the host's order, top-aligned under the total. With fewer than 5, the leftover space stays empty at the bottom of the swap. Rows are never spread apart, so row spacing is identical on every card.
  • More than 5: the 5 highest values, sorted high to low, then "+N more" at the right end of the total line. The full list belongs in the detail view.
  • 0 bars (no breakdown on file): the total, then one caption, "No breakdown on file", in the empty style. The Rest size line shows the size only.
  • Size line: "· {top %} {top bar}" always names the highest value among all the bars supplied, hidden ones included. With 0 bars, or none with a value, it's dropped.
Why "top 5 by value + N more" for more than 5 (option b), not "first 5 in host order" (option a)
  • The card is a scanning surface. What a planner needs from it is where this item is strongest. Option (a) could hide the biggest value just because the host listed it sixth.
  • "+N more" says out loud that something is missing. Option (a) drops data silently, or moves it into an sr-only string sighted users never see.
  • The ≤5 case keeps the host's order, so the usual fixed order (Video, Audio, TV, Social, Forums) still reads by position across the grid. Sorting only kicks in when rows have to be dropped, and then each card's own list is consistently high to low.
  • One change from the suggested form: "+N more" goes at the right end of the total line, not as a caption line under the bars. A sixth line would make the swap taller than the 5-row height and break equal heights. Swapping it in for the fifth bar would show only four. On the total line it costs no height and keeps all five bars.

Equal heights and the Tag limit: measured in your browser

Eight cards at exactly the 280px floor: 1-line, 2-line and over-long titles, next to Tags of 8 to 13 characters. The table below is measured live once fonts load, and again on resize. It shows card heights, title lines, and how much of the title row the title keeps (the target is at least 60%).

Why the limit is 13 characters (measured in Chrome)
  • Measured Tag widths: Sports 58px, Life stage 76px, Shopping 85px, Trendsetter 95px, Income group 108px, Entertainment 112px.
  • Limit: 13 characters. 13 is the longest label in the final list (Entertainment), so every real label is allowed and always shows in full.
  • Cap: 120px, just above the widest allowed label. A label over 13 characters hits the cap and ends in an ellipsis. The full category is still in the Open button's screen-reader name.
  • At the 280px floor the title row is 248px, so the title keeps at least 248 − 8 − 120 = 120px, and more beside shorter labels. Long titles wrap to 2 lines and then ellipsis; the Tag never wraps.

Interaction and accessibility contract

Fixed from the source
  • The source's <article role="button" tabindex="0" aria-label="…"> wrapped a real button. That's invalid nesting. This draft fixes it the way Media Ad Card did: a plain article, the Open action is a real button in the title (stretched over the card), and Add is a sibling button above that hit area.
  • The source's aria-label moves into the Open button's name: visible "Foodies" plus an .sr-only ", Trendsetter, 16M audience. Open details".
  • The source's "Add to reach tray" label didn't name the segment, so every card read the same. The label is now a host string that must name the item and stay fixed (audiences: "Add {Name} to reach tray"). Only aria-pressed changes.
  • The video was already aria-hidden. It's now also preload="none", has no autoplay, and never plays under reduced motion.
Contract (same as Media Ad Card)
  • Live on hover (hover-capable devices only), keyboard focus anywhere in the card, or .is-live after the first tap on touch.
  • Touch: the first tap goes Live, the second opens. The pointer type is read from the click itself, so a keyboard Enter still opens.
  • Tab order: title, then Add. Enter/Space are native. A toggles Add while focus is in the card (no modifiers, no key repeat, never from a text field).
  • Focus is never moved, and nothing is announced: there are no live regions. The pressed state is read from aria-pressed.
  • Bars: the track is aria-hidden. The <dl> reads "Video, 57%" and so on. A missing value reads "Not on file", never blank.
  • Reduced motion: no lift, zoom, tilt, slide, clip or bar growth. The body still cross-fades, and the bars sit at full value.
  • Forced colours: the shipped focus outline applies. Added gets a Highlight outline. Tracks are outlined and fills painted in CanvasText.
Missing data
  • A bar with no value: the row stays. The track is empty and the value shows "—" (.is-empty). A row the host supplied is never removed.
  • Fewer than 5 bars, more than 5, or none: see Bar count.
  • No size: the size line reads "Size not published" (Neutral-5), and the total shows "Not published" in the empty style.
  • No still: the shipped placeholder (as on every card on this page). No clip: leave the video out, and the still just zooms.
  • Long title: the title area is always 2 lines tall, and line 2 ends in an ellipsis. Long tagline: 3 lines, then an ellipsis. Neither changes the card's height.
  • Long category: use this variant's label (13 characters or fewer). The Tag never wraps. A longer label truncates with an ellipsis at the 120px cap.
  • The "· top" part of the size line ignores missing values. If every bar is missing, or there are no bars, the size line shows the size only.

Open questions

  • Toggle naming. Should the shipped -compare / .is-compared get neutral names (e.g. -toggle / .is-picked), so .is-added doesn't copy them and the Add button doesn't reuse a class called "compare"?
  • Life stage and Sports Tag colours. Add a turquoise -bg token + Tag for Life stage? What should Sports use, since red reads as Danger? Both are grey for now.
  • Tagline: 3 lines or 2? 3 fills the taller swap. 2 matches the shipped card but leaves about 40px empty at rest.
  • Hint wording. Keep the shipped "Tap to play", or use the source's "Tap to see"?
  • What Open leads to. No detail view exists yet for audiences (formats open the Media Ad Modal). "+N more" assumes the detail view lists every bar.
  • More than 5 bars. The draft shows the top 5 by value with "+N more" on the total line (option b). Confirm, or prefer the host's first 5 in order (option a)?
  • Name. "Metrics card" with class c-card-media-ad-metrics. The draft file is metrics-card-draft.html.
  • Shipped title clamp. The shipped Media Ad Card probably has the same clamp bug (its clamp sits on an h3 that holds a button). Should the fix go into the shipped card?
