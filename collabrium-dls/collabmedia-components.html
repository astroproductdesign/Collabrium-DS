3

Placement map Page-specific

A laptop and a phone drawn side by side, with the eight places an ad can sit. Each slot shows how many units fit it. On hover the slot fills navy, the others dim, and the slot acts out the placement. Clicking a slot filters the catalogue. Slots are always navy (Water, Collab:Media's element), never a family colour, so a position is never mistaken for a kind of ad. The frames also tilt toward the pointer (not shown here).

Page classes: fm-places · fm-pl-stage · fm-pl-frame · fm-slot · fm-ovsheet

"In the article" is shown selected. Hover any slot to fill it and dim the rest.

7

Format detail modal Recommended

Opens from a card with a swing animation that starts at the card's own position, over a warm backdrop. Contents: breadcrumb with previous/next (← → keys work too), the live sample with Replay, a six-cell spec grid, size swatches drawn to scale against each other, a CTR benchmark bar, and three related-format cards. Missing values are written as "Not on file — ask Sales", never left blank.

Page classes: fm-cardmodal · fm-drawer · fm-specs · fm-sizes · fm-bench · fm-related · fm-relcard
