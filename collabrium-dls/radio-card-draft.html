Radio Card

A choose-one group where each option is a whole card. Radio's semantics and indicator, Card's surface and Selected treatment. Use it for a decision between 2 or 3 paths that each need a sentence of explanation and a live figure.

Anatomy and rules

Every part, its class, and the token it uses. Classes are standalone (c-radio-card) so where it sits, a Radio variant or a Card variant, is still open.

When to use which

ComponentUse when
Radio CardChoose one of 2 or 3 paths, each needs a sentence of explanation or a live figure to decide, and the choice is committed later (form, wizard step, planner). Usually shapes what comes next.
RadioChoose one of up to 4 or 5 options whose labels explain themselves. Lighter, denser, list layout.
Segmented ControlSwitch between views of the same content, applied immediately, short labels (6 characters). Never a form input.
Card: Selectable / SelectedPick many cards for a bulk action elsewhere on the page (checkbox). Radio Card is always exactly one.

Do

  • Keep a group to 2 or 3 cards, all the same anatomy.
  • Wrap the group in a fieldset with a legend that asks the question.
  • Keep descriptions to one or two sentences, and keep meta a short fact.
  • Give a disabled card its reason in the meta slot.
  • Pre-select a card only when there's a real default. Otherwise start empty and validate on submit.

Don't

  • Put buttons, links or other controls inside a card. The whole card is a label.
  • Use it for multi-select (that's Selectable Card), or for immediate view switching (Segmented Control).
  • Add a live region to the meta line, or move focus when it updates.
  • Restyle the circle, or colour a selected card with an element colour (Rule 2).
  • Mark more than one card as Recommended.
