Draft — Progress-to-Goal Bar, nine text elements, no description

All the token suggestions are now in components.css, and the description has been removed from the component, so everything below renders from the shipped stylesheet rather than a draft override — only the greyed “before” cards reconstruct the retired ramp. Each token is taken whole: size, line-height and weight, not just the size. size-small / size-default / size-large now change the chart and nothing else.

1 — The three sizes, in cards

Every word is identical in all three. The bar is 16px, 32px, 40px high at 6, 8 and 10px radius, and the target line’s overshoot is 5, 7 and 9px. Nothing else differs. The head is now just heading + figure — no description line.

5 — Removed from the anatomy

.c-goal-bar-desc is gone, not retokenised. This component states a figure against a target; the sentence explaining why belongs to the surface the bar sits in — a Card’s own description, a PageHeader’s subtitle — not inside the bar. It took the explicit order: 1 / order: 2 on the heading and the figure with it: those existed only to hold a description at order: 3 beneath both, and with two items left in DOM order they were declaring nothing. flex-wrap stays, so at a narrow measure the figure still drops under the heading rather than crushing it. No markup in preview.html carried a description, so the gallery is unaffected.

6 — Five variables retired

--gb-title-size, --gb-desc-size, --gb-legend-size, --gb-footer-size and --gb-tag-size are gone entirely — every reference now names a real token. Two consumer overrides went with them: .c-card-pacing and .c-stat-secondary both forced the legend to caption, which the base now does itself.
